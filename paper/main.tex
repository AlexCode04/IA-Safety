\begin{abstract}
Under a fixed monitoring budget, we measure when access to an agent's
internal state through a linear probe and a Natural Language Autoencoder
adds pre-harm containment value over observable actions and visible
chain of thought. Primary outcomes are pre-harm recall, benign
disruption, expected prevented harm and prevention efficiency.
\end{abstract}
